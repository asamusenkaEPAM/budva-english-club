Your level is enough

No tests. No awkward introductions.

You do not need perfect grammar or a huge vocabulary. Listen when you want, speak when you are ready, and let the conversation do the teaching.

  • Friendly topics and conversation cards
  • A welcoming mix of levels and backgrounds
  • Weekly details posted in the Telegram group

Good to know

Questions before your first meetup?

What level do I need?

Any level is welcome. You can listen first and join the conversation when you feel ready.

Where do you meet?

At cafés and bars in Budva. The current venue and time are posted in the Telegram group.

Can I come alone?

Absolutely. Many people do. The format makes it easy to meet the group and start talking.

Ищете английский клуб в Будве?

Мы встречаемся для живой разговорной практики. Уровень английского не важен — актуальное место встречи публикуется в Telegram.
